Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding paper weights, formatting, numbering, and press options.

What folding styles are available for corporate brochures?

We provide bi-fold (4 panels), tri-fold / letter fold (6 panels), Z-fold, and custom multi-panel accordion folds on creased heavy art card.

What binding is recommended for multi-page product catalogs?

For catalogs up to 48–64 pages, center wire saddle-stitching is standard and cost-effective. For thicker books (60–120+ pages), a glued perfect spine binding provides an executive book finish.

Can you apply spot UV or gold foil stamping on the brochure cover?

Yes. We offer localized spot UV gloss highlighting over matte lamination, as well as metallic gold or silver foil stamping for logos and titles.
