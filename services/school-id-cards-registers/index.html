Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding paper weights, formatting, numbering, and press options.

Are the student ID cards made of real PVC plastic or laminated paper?

Our identity cards are manufactured using 760-micron genuine thermal-fused PVC plastic (same standard thickness and durability as bank ATM cards), not cheap paper laminates.

Can the neck lanyards be custom printed with our school name and logo?

Yes. We manufacture 16mm and 20mm high-density satin lanyards with full-color heat-transfer printing of your school or institutional name and logo.

Can you produce customized register rulings for school attendance and fee logs?

Yes. We produce attendance grids, student fee ledgers, admission registers, and industrial stock registers with custom column rulings and hard rexine case binding.
