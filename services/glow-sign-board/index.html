Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding paper weights, formatting, numbering, and press options.

What is an ACP sheet signboard with 3D acrylic letters?

ACP (Aluminum Composite Panel) provides a sleek, modern architectural background sheet onto which 3D raised acrylic letters are mounted. The acrylic letters are fitted with internal LEDs, creating a high-end illuminated storefront sign.

Are the LED modules waterproof for outdoor monsoon conditions?

Yes. We utilize IP67-rated waterproof LED modules and sealed power drivers specifically manufactured for all-weather outdoor performance.

Can you manufacture both illuminated glowsigns and non-lit ACP boards?

Yes. We fabricate non-lit ACP boards with vinyl or acrylic lettering as well as fully illuminated backlit flex and LED push-through signboards.
